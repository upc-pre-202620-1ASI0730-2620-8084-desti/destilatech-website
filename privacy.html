Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Destilatech we take care of the information of the wineries, liquor stores and distributors that trust us. This policy explains what data we process, why, and what your rights are.

1. Data controller

The FuturosSeniors team, developer of Destilatech as part of an academic project at Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas (UPC).

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, business name and business type (producer or retailer).
  • Operational data: the batches, sensor readings, products, stock, orders, customers and suppliers you record.
  • Preferences: your selected language, stored in your browser.

3. How we use your data

To provide the service, generate alerts and restock estimates, manage your subscription and improve the platform. We do not use your data for advertising.

4. Legal framework

We process personal data in accordance with Peru's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 29733) and its regulations.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your data. We only share it with providers needed to run the service (hosting and payment processing), under confidentiality obligations.

6. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you cancel your account and request it, we delete or anonymize it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss or alteration.

8. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation and opposition (ARCO rights) by contacting the Destilatech team through the platform.

9. Storage in your browser

This website only stores your language preference in your browser. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
